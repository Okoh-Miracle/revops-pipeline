# Project Case Study

## Objective

Streamline inbound lead management, automate follow-up reminders, and centralize sales activity tracking for a growing real estate marketing company.

## Delivery

**Duration:** 3 weeks  
**Role:** Systems / automation implementation  
**Core platform:** Airtable  
**Automation:** Zapier + Make.com  
**Channels:** Instagram, Realtor.com SMS, ListKit, Meta Ads

## Before

Leads were arriving through multiple channels without a unified tracking system. Follow-ups could be delayed or missed, lead quality was not consistently classified, and marketing-channel performance was difficult to review quickly.

## System design

1. **Lead centralization** — lead records from multiple sources were consolidated into Airtable.
2. **Lead classification** — ListKit intent signals could be auto-tagged while the sales process retained Hot / Warm / Cold classifications.
3. **Follow-up logic** — follow-up dates were calculated from first contact and reminders surfaced for BDR action.
4. **Operational SOPs** — Instagram inbox handling and ListKit workflows were structured for a consistent VA → BDR handoff.
5. **Dashboard reporting** — KPIs included lead volume, response rate, time-to-follow-up, meetings booked and conversion rate.

## Documented results

- 80% reduction in manual reporting time.
- Centralized visibility across lead sources and pipeline stages.
- Automated reminders supporting consistent follow-up.
- Manager and BDR views for different day-to-day needs.
